Who This Website Is For

Understanding your visitors helps us show them the right things

Which types of people are most likely to visit your website? Tick all that apply — these are the people you most want to reach.
If the website could only speak to one group perfectly, who would that be?
Think about what questions they would arrive with, or what information would make them stop and read.
Example: "A potential client would want to see our past work, understand what we offer, and know how to get in touch."
Or: "A business partner would want to understand our values, our track record, and how to start a conversation."
A good outcome means they leave the site feeling or doing something positive for your business.

Or: "They call us."  Or: "They save our site to come back when they are ready to proceed."
