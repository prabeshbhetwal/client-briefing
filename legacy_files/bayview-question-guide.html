What your brief already tells us

Bayview Corp needs a premium, modern, clean website for a boutique property development company. The first site should build visibility and trust, include Home, About, Services or Market Focus, Projects, and Contact, and use a contact form for enquiries.

The important scope decision is simple: build a polished website, but make the Projects section editable through a private editing screen. That gives you control over project updates without adding unnecessary complexity.
